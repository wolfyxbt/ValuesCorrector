# Product brand logos

These bundled images require no runtime image API. Both PNGs are 512 × 512,
with a white background and centered artwork padded for circular display.
Brand artwork is preserved without redrawing or stretching.

- `apple.png`: [Apple logo black, Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_logo_black.svg), downloaded September 13, 2026. Original SVG rendered at 360 px tall.
- `kfc.png`: [Primary KFC Logo, official KFC Brand Assets](https://global.kfc.com/media-assets), downloaded September 13, 2026. [Source PNG](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/kbqq3e0r/production/afcc23c7797d1ad1d55bd0e200e2128cfe163eba-4096x3802.png), scaled proportionally to fit 380 × 380 px.

The logos identify their respective brands; trademarks belong to their owners.
